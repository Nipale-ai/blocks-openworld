## 1. Weapons are not held properly

The character carries all three weapons the same way, one-handed, with no real
grip. That reads as wrong immediately, especially in third person where the
character is always on screen.

Each weapon needs its own hold, using both arms:

- **Pistol** — one hand on the grip, the other supporting it, arms bent, weapon
  held at chest height when aiming, lowered across the body when not.
- **Machine gun** — **shouldered**: stock against the shoulder, right hand on
  the grip, **left hand forward on the handguard**, elbow down. Sighting along
  the weapon when aiming, carried at a slant across the chest when not.
- **Rocket launcher** — **on the shoulder**: the tube rests on the right
  shoulder, right hand at the rear grip, left hand at the front grip, head
  tilted slightly to sight along it. This is the one that looks most wrong
  today and the most obvious when it is right.

The rig has `Hand_L` and `Hand_R` and a `WeaponSocket` under `Hand_R`
(see NOTES.md section 5.1). The left hand has to be driven to the weapon's
forward grip — an IK-like solve or a direct pose per weapon, whichever is
simpler to keep stable. Lowered, aiming and firing all need to look right.

## 2. Movement still reads stiff

Round 7 added phase offsets and follow-through. Go further where it shows most:

- **Turning**: the body must lean and rotate into a turn, not pivot on the spot.
- **Start and stop**: a step of acceleration and a settle at the end, not
  instant full speed and instant stop.
- **Sprint**: more forward lean, longer stride, arms driving harder.
- **Strafing and backing up** need their own gait, not the forward one played
  sideways.
- **Landing** from a jump needs a knee bend that absorbs.

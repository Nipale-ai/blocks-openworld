four radio stations as OGG, two screen loops as WebM and MP4, plus
`radio_stations.js`, a base64 variant and a manifest.
**`phase2-medien/NOTES-medien.md` documents exactly how it is meant to load** —
read it first, it was written for this task.

## What to do

1. **The car radio works.** The hook exists and shows "NO SIGNAL". Make R cycle
   the four stations, show the station name in the HUD, keep playing across
   entering and leaving a car, and mute properly with M. Volume should sit
   under the engine and the sirens, not over them — you are driving, not
   listening to a concert.
2. **The screen loops play** where the world has screens or billboards.
3. **Keep the file-system case working.** The game must still run from
   `file://` — that is why the media ships as base64 variants as well. Do not
   break the folder deployment either; both paths must work.
4. **Do not touch anything else.** No gameplay changes, no lighting changes,
   no asset changes. This round is audio and video only.

## Verify

- Play it headless with real Chrome: get in a car, cycle all four stations,
  confirm each actually produces audio (check the analyser or the audio graph,
  not just that no error was thrown), leave the car, get back in.
- Screenshot the HUD with a station name showing, and **look at it**.
- Confirm the screens play their clip.
- Zero console errors, 60 fps unchanged.

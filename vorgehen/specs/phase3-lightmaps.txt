    systema-belegen "model-research" "<section>" <minutes>

Another session needs the card for ~5-minute renders between your sections.
**Take it for 20–30 minutes at a time, release between every section**, and

## Why this round exists

The city is lit at dusk with real-time lights: street lamps, headlights, lit
windows, neon. What real-time lighting cannot do is **indirect light** — the
red glow a neon sign throws on the wall opposite, light bouncing off the road
into the underside of a car, the soft darkening where two walls meet. Cycles
computes exactly that. Baked into textures it costs the player nothing.

This is the single biggest visual upgrade still available.

## What to do

1. **Read the world first.** `../src/world.js` places the city — the street
   grid, blocks, districts, buildings. `../NOTES.md` documents dimensions and
   districts, `../phase2-assets/NOTES-assets.md` the building assets. Rebuild
   that layout in Blender exactly, from the same data, so the bake lines up
   with the game world.
2. **Match the game's dusk lighting.** Read how `../src/main.js` sets sky, sun,
   ambient and fog, and reproduce it in Cycles. A bake lit differently from the
   game looks worse than no bake at all.
3. **Bake per district, in sections** — downtown, midtown/residential,
   industrial/park. Release the lock between sections. Bake indirect light and
   ambient occlusion into the second UV channel the assets were built with.
4. **Denoise, and use sane sample counts.** A noisy bake is worse than none.
   Report samples, times and what you settled on.
5. **Compress the output**: KTX2 via `npx @gltf-transform/cli@latest` or
   `toktx`. These are large textures; uncompressed they will not ship.

## Verify

- Render the baked result and **look at it**. Compare against a screenshot of
  the running game from the same angle. It must be recognisably the same place,
  visibly better lit — not a different city, not blotchy, no seams, no light
  leaking through walls.
- Check that nothing went black, that no district got skipped, and that the
  texel density is even enough that a wall does not look blurrier than the
  pavement.

## Deliver

Into `phase3-lightmaps/out/`: the baked texture sets, compressed, named per
district. Plus `NOTES-lightmaps.md` with:
- one line per file: district, resolution, size, what it covers
- **exact instructions for how the game should load and apply them** — which
  UV channel, which material slot, how to combine with the existing real-time
  lights without double-lighting the scene
- samples, bake times, what you would improve with more time.

Also write the Blender scripts you used into `phase3-lightmaps/` so it can be
repeated after a world change.

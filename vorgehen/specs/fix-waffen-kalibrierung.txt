## The diagnosis (measured, not guessed)

Bone rotations were read from the running game with each weapon selected.
The `HOLDS` poses **are** being applied — the deltas are large:
UpperArm_R 1.86 rad between pistol and rocket, UpperArm_L 2.64 rad,
Hand_R 1.75 rad, Chest 0.40 rad. The code path works.

**The failure is the left arm.** With the rocket launcher the left forearm sits
at −0.188 rad — almost straight — while the upper arm is rotated hard. In the
game the arm sticks out sideways. The owner's words: "the other arm stuck out
to the left". It looks wrong on all three weapons, which is why they read as
identical despite the different numbers.

**Cause:** the anchor/grip offsets in `HOLDS` were estimated, not derived from
the actual weapon geometry. After the anchor rotation is applied, the forward
grip is not where the table assumes it is, so the IK reaches for a point that
forces the arm out and straight.

## 1. Calibrate the holds against the real geometry

Do not guess new numbers. **Measure them.**

The weapon meshes are loaded GLBs. Each has its origin at the grip, the barrel
along +Z, a `muzzle` empty at the tip, and the rocket launcher has grips at
z 0.02 and 0.35 (NOTES.md section 5.3). Read the actual positions out of the
loaded meshes at runtime, transform them through the anchor placement, and
derive where the left hand must go — instead of storing a guessed offset.

Then check, per weapon and per state (lowered and aiming): is the left elbow
bent naturally, is the upper arm close to the body, does the hand actually sit
on the forward grip? A left forearm near 0 rad means the arm is straight and
wrong; it should be clearly bent.

The three holds must be **visibly different from each other**:
- **rocket launcher: tube resting on the right shoulder**, left hand on the
  front grip, elbow down and in front of the chest
- **machine gun: shouldered**, stock at the shoulder, left hand forward on the
  handguard, both elbows down
- **pistol**: both hands together in front of the chest, elbows bent

## 2. Reload animations are missing entirely

`reload` does not appear once in `player.js`. `weapons.js` has the logic
(`startReload`, `reloadT`, `reloading`) and a sound, but the character does
nothing. Add an animation per weapon, driven by the existing reload timer so it
stays in sync:

- **pistol**: magazine out with the left hand, new one in, slide released
- **machine gun**: left hand off the handguard, magazine swap, hand back, charge
- **rocket launcher**: tube tips down off the shoulder, left hand loads a rocket
  at the rear, tube back up on the shoulder

They do not need to be elaborate, but the arms must move and the timing must
match the reload duration.

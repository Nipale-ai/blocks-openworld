## What the game expects

`../NOTES.md` documents a **radio hook** that is already wired: pressing R in a
car cycles stations and currently shows "NO SIGNAL". Read that section and match
what it expects — file names, formats, how many stations. Also check whether the
game has a screen or billboard waiting for a video clip.

## 1. Car radio — several stations

Generate with **ACE-Step** on System A (working example and API details in
`~/pyriq/jarvis-mlops/comfy-raw-api/gen_ace_step.py`; it takes `--tags` and an
optional `--lyrics-file`, so it can sing).

- **Three or four stations, clearly different from each other** — the point is
  that switching stations changes the mood of the drive. Pick genres that suit
  driving through a city at dusk and decide the split yourself; at least one
  station should have **actual vocals**.
- Each track long enough not to feel like a loop on a short drive, and looping
  cleanly — cut on a bar boundary and cross-fade, do not just fade out.
- **VERIFY EVERY TRACK BEFORE ACCEPTING IT.** Render a spectrogram and look at
  it. Silence, truncation and dead ends at the tail are the failure mode here:
  two models before you shipped silent audio because they trusted the generator.
  Also listen to the numbers — peak level, RMS, actual length.
- Encode for the web: OGG or MP3 at a sane bitrate. State the sizes.

## 2. Video for the screens

Generate with **LTX** on System A. One or two short clips that suit a city
billboard or a shop display — something that reads at a glance and loops.
Check the frames before accepting: no collapse to grey, no flicker, no text
turning to mush.

## 3. Deliver it so it can be wired in without guesswork

Everything into `phase2-medien/out/`, plus `NOTES-medien.md` containing:
- one line per file: what it is, length, size, format, loop points
- the station list with names as they should appear in the HUD
- **exact instructions for how the radio code should load and switch them**
- what you generated and discarded, and why

Write the scripts you use into `phase2-medien/` so the work can be repeated.

Do not modify the game. Someone else wires this in afterwards.

Continue the open-world game in this directory. The source is in `src/`, it
builds with `node build.mjs` into `game.html`, and NOTES.md describes every
system. The player has now played it and given feedback. This round is about
acting on that feedback — not about starting anything over.

**DO NOT SSH ANYWHERE. DO NOT USE ANY GPU. DO NOT TRY TO REACH systema, brain,
spark, or ComfyUI.** That machine is in use by someone else. Better models,
textures and baked lighting are a separate later phase; this round is systems
and feel, built locally.

His verdict on what exists: "looks very good already". So do not rewrite what
works. Read the existing code, extend it, and keep everything that is good.

## 1. Police, with a star system — the big one

There is some `cop` handling and a `heat` value in there already. That is not
enough and the name is wrong.

Build a proper wanted system, the way the games this borrows from do it:

- **Stars, one to five, shown as stars in the HUD.** Remove the word "heat" from
  the interface entirely. Stars light up as you commit crimes and cool down over
  time once you break line of sight.
- Crimes escalate you: firing a weapon in public, hitting pedestrians, ramming
  cars, killing, destroying vehicles. Bigger crimes, bigger jumps.
- **Police that actually arrive.** Marked patrol cars that drive to you through
  the street network with **sirens and flashing lights** — the siren is
  synthesised in Web Audio, the lights are real emissive flashes that light the
  street around them at night. You must hear them coming before you see them.
- **Escalation per star level**, and make each level feel different:
  one star a single car that follows and calls it in; two, several cars that try
  to box you in; three, officers on foot who get out and shoot, roadblocks;
  four and five, heavier response — more units, more aggressive driving, pursuit
  that does not simply give up. Design the exact ladder yourself, but each step
  must be recognisably harder than the last.
- Officers on foot must use the existing NPC and weapon systems: cover, aim,
  fire, take damage, die. They are not decoration.
- Losing them must be a skill: break line of sight, get distance, stay out of
  their search area, and the stars drop. Show the cooldown in the HUD so the
  player understands what is happening.

## 2. The driving camera is broken — fix it

His words: while driving, the camera sometimes swings out so far that the car is
no longer visible at all. That is the single worst thing in the build right now.

Suspect: `camera.js` line ~83 lifts the pivot by up to 2.2 m when the camera is
pushed close to the car (`lift = clamp((2.2 - curDist) * 1.1, 0, 2.2)`).
Confirm it yourself before changing anything — do not just take my word for it.

**The rule to hold: the player's own car is always clearly in frame while
driving.** Whatever the camera does to avoid geometry, it never loses the car.
Write a headless test that drives through tight streets, alleys, walls and
collisions, samples where the car is in screen space every frame, and fails if
the car leaves the frame or is occluded for more than a moment. Keep that test.

## 3. Make it feel more dynamic

He wants the game to feel more alive and more kinetic. Choose what serves that
best, but the direction is: more speed, more reaction, more consequence.
Things worth considering — sharper camera response to acceleration and impact,
speed-driven FOV and shake, better hit and damage feedback, more traffic and
pedestrians actually reacting, chases that create chaos in the streets, and
readable but punchy screen effects. Nothing that adds menus.

## Do not break what works

The city, the vehicle physics, the weapon wheel, the character controller and
the frame rate are good. Regressions there cost more than any new feature gains.

## Quality bar — unchanged and still binding

- **Bright enough to see.** Screenshot the running game, measure pixel
  brightness, ground and geometry clearly visible. Under a third near-black.
- Play it headless with real Chrome and look at the screenshots: drive through
  a chase, take stars, get boxed in by police, fight officers on foot. Fix what
  looks wrong and repeat.
- Zero console errors. Hold 60 fps — police units and chases must not sink it.
  Report what you measure.
- Update NOTES.md: the star ladder, what you changed on the camera and why, what
  you added for dynamism, and the phase-2 handover notes kept current.
